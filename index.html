About

Hello! I’m Juhi. Technology consultant. Product thinker. Sailor.

I’m happiest at the intersection of technology, problem solving, and people. Technology consulting and AI work taught me that what I enjoy most is the moment before the build: figuring out what a problem needs, then making it real.

Lately I’ve been chasing that moment earlier: less implementation, more thinking about what should be built, why, and how people will live with it. Off the clock, I’m probably sailing, in a kitchen, or down a curiosity driven rabbit hole.

Not a noun. A verb.
Always becoming.
