About

Technology consultant. Builder. Sailor.

I work at the intersection of technology, problem solving, and people. Through technology consulting and AI work, I realized that the part I enjoy most is figuring out how technology can solve a real user problem.

I want to move from implementing technology to thinking more deeply about what should be built, why it should be built, and how people will actually use it.

The Short Version

I started by designing and developing digital experiences for a real, public audience. From there I moved into enterprise technology and Oracle implementations, then started building AI-powered workflow tools to solve problems I kept seeing repeat themselves.

Along the way I noticed the part of the work I actually gravitate toward isn’t the implementation—it’s the decision before it: what’s worth building, for whom, and why. That’s the direction I’m working toward now.
