About

Hello! I’m Juhi — a Technology Consultant, International Sailing Champion, and Artist.

I grew up sailing competitively in India, traveling for races and learning early how to make decisions when conditions change quickly. Sailing took me across borders, introduced me to people and places I never would have otherwise known, and taught me to adapt, stay curious, and stay calm when conditions change.

I moved to the United States when I was 18, to build a life of my own. Since then, I’ve studied finance and business analytics, worked in technology consulting, built AI tools and digital experiences, and continued sailing, now teaching young sailors on the San Francisco Bay.

Somewhere between technology, sailing, and painting, I’ve realized that I’m happiest when I’m learning, creating, exploring what’s possible, and turning ideas into reality.

Always becoming.
Not a noun. A verb.
