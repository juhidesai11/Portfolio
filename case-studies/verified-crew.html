Verified Crew

Trust & Safety for sailing crew marketplaces

Trust & safety Marketplace design Independent concept
Context

Latitude 38

Latitude 38 is a crew-matching platform that connects boat owners and crew. This concept explores a verification layer I don’t believe currently exists on the platform. It’s my own independent product concept, not a claim about what Latitude 38 offers today.

Problem

Trust is the barrier to joining a boat

When you’re joining a boat with people you don’t know, trust and safety can become a major barrier.

User Concern

A user perspective, not just a hypothesis

I have personal experience sailing and understand the trust problem from a user perspective, both as someone who has had to decide whether to get on a boat with strangers, and as someone who has evaluated crew joining a boat I was on.

Concept

A verification layer for sailing crew

Potential components:

  • Identity verification
  • Optional background check
  • Sailing experience / certification verification
  • References
  • Reviews
  • Previous sailing history
  • Emergency contact
  • Safety / reporting tools

Privacy & Safety Considerations

Requirements, not afterthoughts

Safety and privacy should be treated as product requirements, not afterthoughts.

Any verification layer has to balance real friction (identity checks, background checks) against real risk (getting on a boat with a stranger, sometimes offshore, sometimes for days at a time). That tension is the actual design problem, not a detail to solve later.
