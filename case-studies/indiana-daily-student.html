Editorial Web Design / Indiana Daily Student

Designing for the transition
from print to digital.

As the Indiana Daily Student increasingly relied on digital publishing, the challenge wasn’t simply putting journalism online. We needed to make long-form stories engaging and intuitive for readers experiencing them on a screen, including readers accustomed to the print newspaper.

I co-designed and co-developed two interactive editorial experiences inside an existing publication template, adapting the same technical system to two very different stories.

2 interactive features· HTML / CSS / JavaScript· 10K+ monthly readers
The Challenge

How do you make a newspaper work on a screen?

The publication already had a technical framework for digital stories. That meant I wasn’t starting with a blank canvas. I had to work within an existing system while thinking about reader experience, from scroll flow and image/text pacing to responsive behavior and story-specific UX.

The interesting constraint was that both stories needed to live within the same publication system, but they shouldn’t feel like the same experience.

The Bigger Picture

Publishing content isn’t the same as designing the experience around it.

That experience taught me that the underlying system can stay consistent, while the experience on top of it changes depending on the user and the content.

One of my earliest experiences thinking about the difference between delivering information and designing how someone experiences it.
