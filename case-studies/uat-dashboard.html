The Problem

Testing was spread across many test cases, multiple workstreams, and different owners.

The team needed a quick way to see what was complete, what was still outstanding, where defects were open, and who owned the remaining tests.

What I Built

I structured the existing Xray test scripts by workstream, owner, module, and status, then built filters and a live Jira dashboard for the team to track UAT.
