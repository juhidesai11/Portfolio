SAP S/4HANA Billing Assistant

AI workflow assistant · PwC

Custom GPT Workflow & documentation design Consultant-facing tool
Problem

Billing information was scattered

Project billing information was spread across hundreds of documents and FAQs. Consultants often had to search through large amounts of information to figure out what went wrong or determine how to proceed.

Solution

A contextual, step-by-step guide

I created a Custom GPT that used relevant billing documentation and FAQs to provide contextual, step-by-step guidance.

The assistant could help users across different billing workflows, including:

  • Fixed price billing
  • Milestone billing
  • Monthly billing

A user could also provide a screenshot, allowing the assistant to infer where they were in the workflow and provide relevant next steps.

How I built it

Documentation → instructions → examples → iteration

This was built as a Custom GPT—not a custom-trained model. I supplied relevant information and documents, defined instructions, gave examples of desired outputs, tested the experience, and iterated.

User Experience

What using it felt like

Below is a portfolio representation of the assistant’s conversation style—built with fictional, sanitized example content. It is not a screenshot of internal PwC software.

Billing Assistant (portfolio recreation)
My milestone billing invoice hasn’t generated for Project Atlas. Here’s a screenshot of where I’m stuck.
Thanks for the screenshot—it looks like Milestone 2 hasn’t been marked complete yet, which is what triggers invoice generation. Here’s what to check next:

1. Confirm Milestone 2 approval status
2. Verify the billing schedule matches the SOW
3. Re-run the invoice draft once approved
Got it. What’s different for fixed price vs. monthly billing here?
For fixed price billing, invoices are tied to milestone completion. For monthly billing, invoices generate on a recurring schedule regardless of milestone status. Want the step-by-step for switching a project’s billing type?

Impact

Adopted across teams

The assistant was used by PwC consultants across multiple teams.

This was one part of a broader set of AI workflow initiatives that, together, reduced 400+ hours of manual effort—not a figure specific to this tool alone.

What I learned

Good AI guidance meets people where they are

The most useful version of this tool wasn’t the one with the most information—it was the one that could meet a consultant exactly where they were stuck, screenshot and all.

Designing for real workflows meant thinking less like a document search tool and more like a knowledgeable colleague who already understood the process.
