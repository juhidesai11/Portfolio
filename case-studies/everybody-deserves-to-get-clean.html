‘Everybody Deserves to Get Clean,’ as published on IDS Specials.

The Experience
01

Opening

The piece opens on the same porch where the interview took place, letting a real, specific place carry the headline instead of a stock image.

02

Story flow

The long-form narrative that follows paces first-person testimony against quieter photography, giving a sensitive subject room to breathe instead of competing for attention.

03

Responsive experience

On mobile, the same headline reflows across three shorter lines, so the hero still reads clearly on a five-inch screen.
