The Story

Built so I’d stop repeating myself

I built a Wix site as a cookbook/repository so friends and family could cook my recipes without having to repeatedly ask me for them.

Target users: friends and family, and people learning to cook from scratch.

Product goal: make recipes easy to find, understand, and actually cook.
