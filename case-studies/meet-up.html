Product Concept | Maps

Meet there, together.

Maps helps friends coordinate when to leave and arrive at the same time, so plans feel easier and less chaotic.

Same destination.
Less back and forth.

The Users

Friends meeting at the same place from different starting points.

People making plans together who are traveling separately and trying to arrive around the same time.

The Problem

Getting there together takes too much coordination.

Once a plan is made, coordination moves into the group chat. People ask who’s left, share ETAs, send location updates, and adjust as plans change.

Have you left yet? I’m 10 mins away ETA says 7:08 Where are you guys? Wait should I leave now?

Everyone knows where they’re going.
The hard part is coordinating when to get there.

Potential Solutions
01

Group chat coordination

Everyone shares ETAs manually.

Already familiar, but requires constant updates.

02

Live location sharing

Everyone shares where they are.

Useful for visibility, but exact location doesn’t necessarily help people coordinate when to leave.

03

Coordinated meetup

Maps uses everyone’s route and ETA to recommend individual leave times.

Helps the group coordinate around the actual goal: arriving together.

I explored the third direction because it uses information Maps already understands, including routes, travel time, and traffic, to solve the coordination problem directly.

The Concept

Meet there, together.

Set a destination and time. Maps works backward from everyone’s journey to help the group arrive around the same time.

Key Product Decision

Do friends need to share their exact location?

Not necessarily. If the goal is coordinating arrival, an ETA and arrival status may be enough. Exact location sharing can remain optional.

How I’d Ship It

Start with the smallest useful version.

The mockup shows the larger vision. I would first test whether people actually want help coordinating when to leave.

    Prototype
