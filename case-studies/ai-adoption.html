Teaching 30+ professionals to build with AI

AI Adoption & Enablement · PwC

Training design Workshop facilitation Change management
Context

Building the tools was only part of the work

Building AI tools was only part of the work. I also focused on helping people understand how to use AI effectively and confidently.

Generative AI was evolving quickly, but knowing that AI exists isn’t the same as knowing how to use it well. I wanted to help my team move from simply experimenting with AI to applying it to real business problems.

The Journey

Learn → Practice → Apply → Reinforce

01

Learn

Designed and delivered AI training sessions covering AI fundamentals, practical applications, and everyday use cases.

02

Practice

Created hands-on workshops where 30+ PwC professionals could experiment with AI tools and work through practical use cases.

03

Apply

Designed an AI case competition where teams applied what they learned to real business problems.

04

Reinforce

Encouraged continued experimentation, peer learning, and practical adoption.

Why This Is Product Work

Adoption is a product problem too

Understanding user needs, reducing friction, communicating clearly, and helping people change behavior are all part of creating something useful.

This work drew on the same muscles as building a product: reading a room of varied technical comfort, designing an experience that met people where they were, and iterating the format based on what actually landed.

  • User-centric learning design
