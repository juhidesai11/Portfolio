AI Adoption Program

Helping teams learn how to use AI in their day-to-day work.

The Problem

People had access to AI tools, but many weren’t sure how to use them.

Some PwC employees were unfamiliar with the tools available to them, while others weren’t sure where AI could actually help in their work.

Solution

I designed eight hands-on sessions where people could learn the tools, try them themselves, and apply them to examples from their work. The program ended with a live case competition where teams put what they’d learned into practice.

The Sessions

The sessions covered AI fundamentals and hands-on practice with 7 tools, including how to choose the right model for a task.

Case Competition

The AI Build-Off

A 90-minute case competition where teams built AI solutions to Oracle Finance problems.

Oracle Finance Challenges

  • EPM close cycles
  • FCCS reconciliations
  • EBS-to-Cloud migrations
  • ARCS exceptions
How It Worked

Three parts

The Brief

Each team worked on a real Oracle Finance implementation scenario.

The Build

Teams used Claude and/or ChatGPT Custom GPTs to build a working AI solution in 90 minutes.

The Demo

Teams ran their solutions live in front of judges.

How It Was Scored

Scoring

Judges scored five weighted categories, with bonus points for advanced techniques.

01
AI tool depth & breadth
How many of the 7 tools were used, and used well.
35 pts
02
Problem actually solved
Accurate, credible, and addressed the brief.
25 pts
03
Prompt engineering
Role, format, constraints, hallucination guards.
20 pts
04
Live demo execution
Did it work live, clearly and confidently?
12 pts
05
Wow factor
Something genuinely surprising or polished.
8 pts

Bonus: +5 pts each for an MCP cross-app chain, a Cowork autonomous run, or client-ready output, up to +15 pts.

Impact

The sessions covered when and how to use each tool. In the Build-Off, teams had to choose the right model or tool for the problem in front of them.
