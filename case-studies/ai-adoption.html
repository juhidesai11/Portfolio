The Problem

People had access to AI tools, but many weren’t sure how to use them.

Some PwC employees were unfamiliar with the tools available to them, while others weren’t sure where AI could actually help in their work.

What I Did

I designed eight hands-on sessions where people could learn the tools, try them themselves, and apply them to examples from their work. The program ended with a live case competition.

The Sessions

Eight hands-on workshops

From AI fundamentals and prompting to choosing the right tool for the task.
