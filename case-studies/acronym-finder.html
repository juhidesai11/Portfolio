PwC Acronym Finder

Conversational knowledge search · PwC

Custom GPT Information architecture Firm-wide tool
Problem

A multi-page reference, hard to search

PwC had a large multi-page source of acronyms. Finding the meaning of a specific acronym quickly was cumbersome.

Iteration

Test → Observe → Iterate → Improve

Getting this right took four rounds of restructuring the underlying information, not the model.

Version 1

WebsiteMany pages, hard to search

Version 2

Extracted to CSV
→
Poor retrievalThe Custom GPT struggled to retrieve accurate results

Version 3

Converted to Word docs
→
Still inconsistentLarge documents didn’t consistently produce accurate responses

Version 4

Split into 4 groupsA–E · F–L · M–R · S–Z
→
Improved retrievalFaster, more accurate acronym lookups
What I built

A conversational search experience

I transformed a multi-page knowledge source into a conversational search experience.

Impact

Made available firm-wide

The tool was made available across the firm and used by PwC employees.

What I learned

Structure is the product

Information architecture directly affects AI UX.

Building an AI experience isn’t only about the model or the interface. How the underlying information is structured shapes whether the whole thing actually works.
