New Joiner Guide

AI onboarding assistant · PwC

Custom GPT Tone & UX design Onboarding
Problem

Onboarding didn’t end when training did

PwC onboarding included a large amount of information delivered through training sessions. After onboarding, new employees still needed quick answers about processes, resources, and who to contact.

Solution

An assistant built from onboarding materials

I created a Custom GPT using the onboarding PowerPoints from a two-week in-person training program, along with related onboarding resources and links.

It could answer questions such as:

  • Where can I find my paystub?
  • How do I charge my time?
  • Who should I contact about career progression?
  • What should I do if I need leave?
  • Where can I find onboarding resources?
Design Decision

Tone was a deliberate choice

I intentionally designed the assistant to respond in a motivating and encouraging tone because the users were new employees.

Getting the right answer was the baseline. Getting it right in a way that made a nervous new hire feel supported was the actual design problem.

Impact

Incorporated into onboarding

The New Joiner Guide was incorporated into PwC onboarding and used by new joiners.

What I learned

The right answer isn’t always enough

Useful AI isn’t only about giving the right answer. The tone, context, and moment in which the answer is delivered can shape whether users actually want to use it.
